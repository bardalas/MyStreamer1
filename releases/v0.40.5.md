## VEO 0.40.5

- Android system bars no longer overlay the app. The page is kept inside the space the status and
  navigation bars leave (including the side one, held sideways), and those strips are painted in the
  skin's night colour instead of the WebView's default white.
- On a phone, episodes render as a compact full-width list instead of inheriting the desktop card's
  200-300px width as each item's height. Seasons remain a horizontal selector. TV layout is unchanged.
